USNexa Business Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

USNexa Business ("USNexa Business", "we", "us", or "our") is a business-management and point-of-sale application operated by USNexa. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed when you use the USNexa Business app.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, USNexa Business may process:

  • Account information: information needed to create and authenticate a business account, such as an email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Business information: business name and other business details entered by the business owner or authorized users.
  • Employee information: names, employee numbers, phone numbers, job titles, employment dates, attendance, leave, promotions, terminations, rehires, and related records entered by an authorized business user.
  • Product and inventory information: product names, SKUs, prices, costs, stock quantities, stock movements, and related records.
  • Sales and transaction information: products sold, quantities, prices, payment method, cash received, change, receipt numbers, transaction dates and times, returns/refunds, and related business records.
  • Customer information: customer information that a business user chooses to enter, including information used for customer records or loyalty features.
  • Business operations information: expenses, suppliers, cash-drawer events, business activity records, and other operational information entered into the app.
  • Technical information: information necessary for authentication, security, troubleshooting, and reliable operation of the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate the app, authenticate users, manage business records, process sales and inventory operations, maintain transaction and employee histories, provide search and reporting features, support security, troubleshoot problems, and improve the reliability of the service.

3. Cloud storage and service providers

USNexa Business uses third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase, to provide authentication, database, and cloud services. Information submitted to the app may therefore be processed by these providers on our behalf. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

4. Payments

USNexa Business can record business payment methods such as cash, card, and EFT for sales records. The app is not intended to collect or store customers' complete card numbers, PINs, passwords, or banking credentials. Payment processing, where applicable, is handled through the relevant payment provider or financial institution rather than by storing complete payment credentials in USNexa Business.

5. Sharing of information

We may share or disclose information only as necessary to provide the service, operate our infrastructure, protect the service and its users, comply with applicable law, or respond to lawful requests. Business owners and authorized users are responsible for ensuring that information they enter about employees and customers is collected and used lawfully.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

Business records may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain accounting and operational history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention may vary depending on the type of record and the business account's use of the service.

8. Account and data deletion

If you want to request deletion of your USNexa Business account and associated personal data, contact us at tranquilsboh@gmail.com with the account email address and a clear request for account/data deletion. We will process eligible deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our legitimate record-retention obligations.

9. Children's privacy

USNexa Business is a business-management application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of operating a child-directed service.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
